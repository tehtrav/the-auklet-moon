🌙 Moonfur's Guide

"Greetings, young one! I am Moonfur of StarClan. Let me show you around OceanClan's home."

Places to find

  • The Highrock in the middle of camp. Jump up the stepping stones to reach the top!
  • The secret tunnel in front of the Highrock leads down to the Leader's Cave.
  • The dens: Warriors', Apprentices', Elders' and the Nursery. Walk inside!
  • The beach and ocean: OceanClan cats can swim. Try it!
  • Tide pools and sea caves along the shore.

Day and night

A whole day and night takes 20 minutes. Watch the sun rise over the ocean and the moon come out at night. Hold T to make time go faster.
